# Heist and Escape Minigame — Provisional Idea

Subject to change to fit the broader story, crew, and final infiltration/extraction sequence.

A short stealth minigame inspired by GFL2’s Boundary Push, serving the narrative in the manner of the game’s other brief minigames. Focus on a few engaging, deliberately arranged encounters rather than an extensive standalone game.

The core pleasure is chess-like: read overlapping patrols and supporting positions, find exposed pieces, and peel back the defenses in layers. Offer several approaches to objectives: bypass, distract, disable, or eliminate. Clearing enemies can be satisfying but should not be required. Some guards cover ordinary space rather than key objectives.

Crew members provide different ways to change the situation. Langley is the main fighter; the MC may also fight, depending on the story. Other characters can draw guards away, create access, or perform other supporting functions. Sneaking through with Kathay is one possible approach. Temporary distractions matter where a permanent solution would have unacceptable consequences.

Depth should come from combining a small number of distinct character abilities with the defensive layout. Avoid a single universally best ability or one prescribed solution. Exact abilities, controls, consequences, and whether infiltration and escape reuse a map remain undecided.

Next step: coordinate with the narrative before fixing mechanics, character roles, mandatory events, or outcomes.
