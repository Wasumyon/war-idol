===== Chip's Concert =====

The crowd is a mesmerizing sea of softly glowing cherry blossom light-sticks. It explodes into a whirlwind of petals calling forth:

“oooo~OOH! Give us the War Idol! HEY HEY! oooo~OOH! Give us the War Idol! HEY HEY!”

I stand two paces behind her, framing her avatar, steady-cam rig heavy on my shoulder. Through my lens, she is without flaw. Her digital twintails bounce in a perfect rhythm with her race horse gait. She smiles a high-fidelity smile that cost her agency millions to render.

A boy breaks out of the crowd and bounds the velvet ropes. Chip bends gracefully at the hip to meet the surprise, her neon avatar leaning forward to rustle the hair of the young man. I blink, adjusting the focal length on my lens. My bare eyes see a child in an oversized costume, pauldrons drooping over his small frame, but my mind knows better. 

He's a young man, I remind myself. A proper honourable recruit. He's old enough to defend his home.

“Playing soldier, sweetie?”
He puffs up his cheeks and bats the hand away, impetuous, but gentle.
“I’m a E-2 now! I hated school and had nothing to do until I saw you on the Feed! I’m doing my part to crush those bugs!”
The boy looked like he had never even nicked on a razor, much less seen blood. His immaculate face is free from sulfur scars and ammo grease. 
The recognition punched Chip in the plexus, but she recovered.
“O-oh! That’s… That’s my brave soldier! Don’t forget to keep supporting your favourite War Idol!” 
“I’m a gold member! Remember D4rK-P4l1id4n? Those are my super chats!”
I heard the breathing change and be instantly edited out by filters. I felt her heart sink, but Chip was always pro first, big sister last. 
“Remember to tune in Saturday, I’ll give you a special song request”
“On my first- er my next deployment! It’ll be my good luck charm. Do you know “One More Time?”
“One more time~ We’re going to celebrate~ Oh Yeah~! Big sis will keep you safe out there!”

She drops down low, her avatar’s face inches from his actual, human face. The boy’s ears flush a violent, burning red. With a wave of her hand she shuts off her microphone feed, leaning directly into his ear to whisper a line that the mob will never hear:

“Stay alive, okay? Or else Chip is going to make fun of you forever.”

Then he gives him a kiss on the forehead. He will never forget this day. He stares at her as she smiles and waves and walks to the next guest.
