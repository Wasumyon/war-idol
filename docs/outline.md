# War Idol — Master Story Outline & Scene Roadmap

**What this file owns:** the beats, in order. The acts and the endings are the bible's
(`io_story_bible.md`, "Structure"); day placement is `maingame_spec.md` §2; open story questions
are in `punchlist.md` §B. Beats marked *proposed* fill a gap the bible leaves and wait on a yes.

## Structure Overview
The story follows the narrator (an amputee veteran seeking legitimacy and absolution) and Chip (a broke, neurotic VTuber seeking viral popularity as armor against abandonment) as they navigate the counterinsurgency media-industrial complex on Io.

- **Act 1: The protective illusion (Beats 01–16, 0–25%)** — Chaac, the rejection, Keno's, the contract, Day 1's stream; Aysel and Langley; the red flag; Kathay's stream and the LZ.
- **Act 2: The cracking facade (Beats 17–33, 25–75%)** — Albrecht's rescue, Ottenok's trap and death, the ice, the gala, Aysel's vendetta, the EMP, the visor falls, the capture.
- **Act 3: The interrogation and the fork (Beats 34–40, 75–100%)** — Albrecht's one scene, the fork; Route A's breakout, heist and broadcast; Route B's epilogue.

Beats 01–10 are Day 1 and are written (`game/scenes/00_`–`04_`). The beat numbers changed in the
three-act restructure; the old-to-new map is at the end.

## Act 1: The protective illusion

Running through the act, in free slots and scene texture: the popular worship of Albrecht through
money, pamphlets and soundbites (bible, Act 1).

### Beat 01: Prologue — Battle of Chaac Patera *(written: `00_prologue`)*
- **Setting:** The verdigris sulfur pits of Chaac Patera.
- **Action:** Combat drop. The APC is ripped open. The narrator is blown clear, suffering traumatic loss of his right leg. In the sensory overload and HUD glitch, he catches an unrendered glimpse of what he's firing at before blacking out.
- **Payoff:** Seeds that the war on Io is fundamentally wrong. Establishes narrator's loss, survival count (four landed, one died in rehab, three alive), and physical disability.

### Beat 02: Rejection — The Recruiter's Office *(written: `01_intro`)*
- **Setting:** Ministry Enlistment Office, High Juno.
- **Action:** Narrator attempts reenlistment to return to the field. The recruiter—a tired bureaucrat who knows the Chaac record—flatly rejects him on the grounds of his crude prosthetic leg ("with *that*?") and points him at Vulkan Security and the mercs. He about-faces and lands at the foot of the concrete steps.
- **Mechanical Hook:** Mention of his brother; the pension payout is revealed to be a quiet settlement meant to keep him out of sight.

### Beat 03: The Foot of the Stairs — The Great Red Eye *(written: `01_intro`)*
- **Setting:** The red pavement outside the recruiting office, then the Juno alleys.
- **Action:** Nobody helps him up. Internal monologue: the Pioneer bubble, the Revelation starting at Argos, the street version of the Infestation — suits boring into bodies, the horns, the water rumour.
- **Note:** The earlier plan to give this to a moon-touched beggar is dropped here. The bible's beggar is the inciting red flag, Beat 13.

### Beat 04: The Bar — Captain Keno's *(written: `01_intro`)*
- **Setting:** Captain Keno's Bar, an ex-military dive catering to veterans, logistics runners, and low-tier mercs.
- **Action:** Narrator goes to drink off his refusal. Keno serves him. Chip arrives—loud, hyperactive, wearing street gear, broadcasting to a failing channel. Keno presents the riddle of the four drinks (*The Four Jewels of Io* / *The Flight*): White Cow, Peacock, Gadfly, Metamorphosis. Narrator observes Chip drinking without grasping the ceremony.

### Beat 05: The Alley — The Badge and the Blood *(written: `01_intro`)*
- **Setting:** Dark alley behind Keno's.
- **Action:** Chip is cornered and assaulted by local goons/punks. Narrator intervenes with his cane and las-pistol; kills two attackers with brutal, practiced efficiency.
- **The Twist:** Chip wasn't screaming for rescue; she was filming. She flashes her Ministry Media Tick (Badge of Journalistic Authority). The violence sends viewer count and donations spiking through the ceiling.
- **Aftermath:** Narrator scolds her for risking her neck for numbers; she counters that being seen is the only insurance that keeps you alive.

### Beat 06: The Contract — Mutual Ruin *(written: `01_intro`)*
- **Setting:** Back room / booth at Keno's.
- **Action:** They draft their operational pact over cheap rations: fifty-fifty, profit and expense.
- **The Terms:**
  1. *Elbow out the competition:* Climb the Feed rankings by any means necessary.
  2. *The Death Clause:* If either dies in the field, all gear, broadcast rights, and assets revert to the survivor.
- **Themes:** Legitimacy meets monetization. He gets his ticket back into the red zone; she gets lethal muscle that won't run.

### Beat 07: First Stream (In Media Res) *(written: `02_stream`)*
- **Setting:** Red Zone perimeter, moving at high velocity.
- **Action:** Thrown directly into the combat broadcast. Chip hypes the feed while the narrator manages defenses, dodging railgun debris and incoming missile locks while framing high-action combat shots.
- **Tutorial:** Half-tutorial. Chip and the narrator teach the controls in banter, each line fired by an event (first dodge, three dodges, first beam). They bicker over the rig's name: he calls it "Isabella", she calls it "The Big Chip".
- **Cliffhanger:** An undodgeable guided warhead locks onto the vehicle. "The Big Chip is going down!" Flashback cut.

### Beat 08: Flashback — The Loan, The Mechie & The Rig *(written: `03_car`)*
- **Setting:** Tank's Mechie Garage, Lower Juno.
- **Action (Earlier that Day):**
  - Meet **Tank**: stubborn, grease-stained mechanic fighting to keep an independent repair shop alive against corporate rentiers and Ministry hardware monopolies.
  - The broadcast vehicle: a battered, government-surplus APC retrofitted with heavy glass, camera gantry, and dangerous broadcast rigs.
  - The narrator secures a predatory high-interest loan to patch the cooling lines and install armor plates.
  - The rig's name: still argued, never settled.
  - Possible cameo: Langley snooping for her dad.
  - Integration: Narrator calls in an old favor to mount a salvaged APC blast engine recovered from the wreckage of Chaac Patera (boost vs super-armor trade-off).

### Beat 09: Resolution of the Strike *(written: `02_stream` / `03_car`)*
- **Setting:** Return to Beat 07 cliffhanger.
- **Action:** Narrator dumps power into the salvaged blast engine/super-armor. The missile detonates off the reinforced deflector; the van barrels through smoke. The stream viewers erupt; superchats flood the HUD.
- **The name:** After boosting through the final obstacle, the narrator claims naming rights: "I saved your life on the ___, and that's that!" The player fills the blank and names the rig. Chip concedes. (Placement is still open: `HANDOVER.md`.)

### Beat 10: Aftermath at Keno's *(written: `04_mission_clear`)*
- **Setting:** Captain Keno's Bar.
- **Action:** Celebrating Day 1 survival and revenue split. Chip orders the flight again, demonstrating she has begun to understand Keno's drink riddle—acting less like a desperate tourist and more like an operator.

### Beat 11: Enter the Rival — Aysel Terpsichore
- **Setting:** Keno's Bar / Feed monitors.
- **Action:** The screens light up with the #1 ranked War Idol stream: **Aysel Terpsichore**. She broadcasts high-production, sadistic footage torturing captured "bugs" on camera, framing it as patriotic duty for the 8 Families.
- **Confrontation (Aysel is in the bar in person):**
  - **Kathay speaks first** — a loud stranger at the bar; the crew doesn't know her yet. She jabs at Aysel's ties to the elite and the title and pose "handed" to her by Albrecht, and may compliment up-and-coming Chip.
  - Aysel moves to strike her; Kathay flinches into a cute defensive crouch. Aysel retracts the fist and scoffs: Albrecht took charge and saved the people, there'd be no Captain Keno's without him, no shame in helping a man like that. She flaunts her wealth and calls people like Chip lookie-loos after a quick buck and cheap thrills (true of herself too; left unsaid). The pro-Albrecht men at the bar cheer and hoot.
  - **Chip**, mortified, fires back in desperation: Aysel's a sell-out, nothing without her money and connections.
  - **Kathay** drops a conspiracy that's close to the truth but a red herring: Aysel and Albrecht are in on it together. A third faction, not anti-Albrecht but sick of people throwing their weight around (Stormcloaks vs Empire), calls the cheering side bootlickers.
  - Kathay then lets slip what happened to the Terpsichore family and Aysel's sudden rise. Huge reaction from the rebellious crowd.
  - **Aysel loses it and swings.**
- **Introduction of Langley:** A tall, deadpan merc enforcer who has been watching the whole time (foreshadowed in the art as a literal shadow). She steps in, takes Aysel's punch on the cheek, and just stares at her. She watches Chip's feed; her compliments sound like insults, but she reveals genuine tactical insight. Suffers from stage fright when facing a camera, but zero battle fright under fire. Recruitable as automated defensive gunner.
- **The brawl:** The bar erupts. Captain Keno calmly dodges, tallying who broke what and who to charge, with superhuman comic calm — another Saturday. Kathay scurries off. Aysel, shamed, declares war on Chip and her crew (just the two of them so far) and marches out as bottles and glasses fly over her as if she has a protective bubble. The MC just enjoys the catfight, to Chip's consternation.
- **Choice note (for later):** a good spot for "fake" choices that don't change the story — comic participation and effect.

### Beat 12: The Interview System & Watcher 6
- **Setting:** The Big Chip / Mobile Rig.
- **Action:** Tank installs a repurposed military ham radio / long-range intercept array.
- **The Scoop:** Chip conducts an audio interview with a ragged voice claiming to be the sole survivor of the fallen outpost *Watcher 6*. The emotional, raw broadcast breaks algorithmic records.
- **Undercurrent:** Unknown to Chip, the voice belongs to Langley's missing father.

### Beat 13: The Inciting Red Flag — The Moon-Touched Veteran
- **Source:** bible, Act 1; the moon-touched soldier, canon §8.
- **Setting:** A Juno street or a front-line rest stop, on a run.
- **Action:** A moon-touched homeless veteran raves that the monsters are just men — "They're not bugs!", the teeth. Chip is filming. The narrator shoves money into his hand to shut him up, and gets a violent spike of phantom pain (canon §4).
- **Payoff:** The first time the body answers for what the visor glosses. He tells himself it's the cold.

### Beat 13A: The Collector — Fine Print *(proposed; placement open, days 2–6)*
- **Source:** the author, 2026-11-15. The loan is Beat 08's. Mechanics: `stealth_spec.md` §5.
- **Setting:** The garage or Keno's, after the first payment on the loan has been made.
- **Action:** The collector comes anyway. He shows a slipped-in clause: legalese, compound interest, math jargon. The narrator moves to beat him; the collector stays completely calm. The narrator has him by the collar with one hand and jabs his pistol into his stomach with the other.
- **Open:**
  - **Whose loan.** The draft has Chip borrowing from shady people who come to collect. `03_car` (Beat 08, written) has the narrator take the predatory loan for the rig. Same loan with Chip as co-signer, or a second one of hers?
  - **"Dekaros"** is not in the bible or canon, and the player names the narrator at New Game (canon §8).
  - **Automatons** are not in the bible or canon. A man who is really a machine sits close to the twist; it needs a canon line.
  - The `LOANSHARK` flag in `game_design.md` describes a different collector (hacks the computer, damages the car).
- **Draft lines** (the author's, not yet edited against the style guide):
  - Collector: "Sure Dekaros. You can destroy me, that is your function. In addition, you are quite able at it. But what good will that do you?" He removes his head, showing that he is an automaton, then snaps it back on.
  - Collector: "You call us usurers. Yes, we are. We exist because people require our services. Business, my dear, hasn't changed in a million years. It won't in a million either."
  - Narrator: "You call this business? Rip off a greenhorn" (gesturing to Chip) "and then what? Sell her organs. Why not drink her blood while you're at it?" He pushes him and releases his grip, but never takes his gun or his eyes off the collector's vitals.
  - Collector: "We're usurers, not devils."
  - Narrator: "What's the difference?"
  - The collector unzips and reaches into his jacket. The narrator tenses. He pulls out a holodisc. It shows the solar system, then Jove, then Io, then an area on it, then the schematics of a base.
  - Collector: "It is in the subsection of that clause, you and your partner's services we find... unconventional and brash."
  - Chip: "Thanks!"
  - Collector: "Go to these coordinates, put your... rare abilities to my master's use, and we'll forgive this... mistake."
  - Narrator, scoffing: "Devils make deals too. Ok, but this is the last time, you scam artist."
  - Collector, dusting off his cloak: "Next time, get someone to read the fine print."
  - Chip: "Looks like we made a new fan!"
- **Payoff:** The base on the holodisc is the first stealth map, the narrator and Chip only. Finishing it clears the debt and opens the dirt-collecting job in the hub.

### Beat 14: Content First — Kathay's Banned Stream (Day 5, evening)
- **Source:** canon §11, Kathay's order of arrival.
- **Setting:** The rig or Keno's, after the Day 5 checkpoint.
- **Action:** Chip pulls up a banned conspiracy stream. The loud stranger from Beat 11 is a broadcaster: **Kathay**, bombastic, talking over her callers, selling supplements and Faraday helmets. Close to the truth and wrong about all of it.
- **Payoff:** She is a name before she is a body. The rescue in Beat 16 means nothing without this.

### Beat 15: The Broker's Leak
- **Setting:** Transit routes through the sulfur plains.
- **Action:** Information broker **Ottenok** sells the crew an exclusive leak on an unannounced Ministry staging zone, opening lucrative new high-risk broadcast routes.

### Beat 16: A Body Second — Kathay at the LZ (Day 7)
- **Source:** canon §11. *Conditional* on two investigation segments; the shared fallback is Beat 18.
- **Setting:** A landing zone on the edge of a patrol route.
- **Action:** Following her stream's breadcrumbs, the crew tracks Kathay to an LZ as she blunders toward a patrol after suppressed Ministry data. They pull her out. She is exactly as loud in person.
- **Act break:** The Day 10 checkpoint closes Act 1.

## Act 2: The cracking facade

### Beat 17: Too Deep — Albrecht's Rescue
- **Source:** bible, Act 2.
- **Setting:** A combat zone past the line, on one of Ottenok's new routes *(proposed)*.
- **Action:** The crew pushes too deep for views and gets cornered. **Albrecht personally intervenes** and pulls them out. Loyalty earned: the narrator's legitimism finally has a face.
- **Albrecht's lines:** cryptic, double-meaning hints that read as grace now and as confession later. He argues, never enforces (canon §13). No self-justification here: that is spent once, in Beat 34.

### Beat 18: Ottenok's Trap
- **Setting:** Sub-crustal mining bypass / lava tubes.
- **Action:** The tip was a death trap set to eliminate independent streamers. The crew arrives to find an ambush in progress.
- **Kathay:** She is already known (Beats 14, 16). She bought the same coordinates looking for suppressed Ministry data, and is caught in the same ambush.
  - *If Beat 16 was missed (fallback):* this is where the crew meets her in person, from her stream. Written so a player who saw Beat 16 never meets a line that assumes they didn't, and the reverse.
- **The Passage:** Under heavy fire, they stash the damaged vehicle in an abandoned deep-bore tunnel and escape on foot through caverns filled with petrified/dead "bugs."
- **Kathay's Theory:** While hiding, Kathay dumps her conspiracy lore: Albrecht's 40-year exile, the power-sharing deadlock of the 8 Founding Families, and her wild theory that the creatures are "Nephilim angels" culled from Jupiter's Eye via ancient occult prions. (Narrator listens with quiet skepticism).
- **Stealth:** Sneaking past a dormant cluster of miners/creatures before reaching Ottenok's bunker.

### Beat 19: Ottenok Dead
- **Setting:** Ottenok's fortified communication relay.
- **Action:** They breach the relay to find Ottenok executed at his terminal.
- **Evidence:** Left behind is a customized Terpsichore sigil pistol, planted to make it look like Aysel killed him. The Ministry did it, because he was touching the truth.
- **The locker:** the team finds Aysel hidden in a locker, a witness Ottenok put there with something only Kathay can later crack. Gun up, shaking, then she bolts. The Ministry tells her the team did it. Detail: canon §8.
- **Kathay:** With Ottenok dead, his chair is empty. She takes it (Beat 26).

### Beat 20: The Ice Towers
- **Setting:** The frozen methane and sulfur ice pillars, two days out from Juno.
- **Action:** With the rig compromised and systems freezing, the crew navigates the treacherous terrain on foot and flatbed.

### Beat 21: Ambush of the Ice Raiders
- **Setting:** Narrow crevasse between the ice spires.
- **Action:** Ice Desert Raiders (desperate, unlicensed outcasts living beyond the dome networks) surround and ambush the party.

### Beat 22: Langley's Reveal
- **Setting:** Raider kill-zone.
- **Action:** As bandits grab Chip, a warning shot cracks from the ridge. One raider steps forward, lowering a snow visor: **Langley**. She is embedded with/held by the bandits.

### Beat 23: Captives by Choice
- **Setting:** Raider dugout / cave settlement.
- **Action:** To prevent a bloodbath, Langley stages their capture, agreeing to sit in holding cells with them. She explains she tracked the bandit network following leads on her father's distress transmission. She plays the recording: her father's voice, desperate and cut short by Ministry jammers.

### Beat 24: The Geothermal Haven
- **Setting:** Deep cave volcanic spring (onsen) beneath the ice.
- **Action:** Downtime beat. The crew washes sulfur dust away, thawing out frozen prosthetics and sharing histories. Contrasts the freezing Jovian waste with human warmth.

### Beat 25: Caravan Raid & The Breakout
- **Setting:** Military supply convoy route.
- **Action:** A Terpsichore private security convoy moves through raider territory. The bandits attack; the raid turns chaotic when heavy gunships intervene.
- **The Break:** The narrator, Chip, and Langley use the firefight to hijack an armored hauler and escape back toward Juno, securing shipping manifests proving Aysel is stockpiling heavy kinetic boosters.

### Beat 26: Reorganizing the Crew
- **Setting:** Tank's Mechie Garage.
- **Action:** The crew consolidates. Langley signs on as primary tactical enforcer. The narrator is formally designated **Executive Producer**, controlling operations, stream routes, and asset allocation.
- **Kathay:** She does not join the crew. She takes over Ottenok's position as the info dealer — badly (bible). She sells to the crew like anyone else, oversells, gets things wrong, and is the leak point Aysel will use in Beat 30.

### Beat 27: Infiltrating the Terpsichores
- **Setting:** Upper Tier Spire, Juno Grand District.
- **Action:** Disguised using counterfeit media credentials, the team infiltrates a lavish gala hosted at the Terpsichore family manor.

### Beat 28: The Sabotage & The Hardware
- **Setting:** Aysel's private server suite.
- **Action:** Kathay cracks the estate network from her chair while Chip runs social interference. The narrator infiltrates Aysel's sanctum.
- **The Discovery** *(proposed)*: the hardware, not the plan. Ministry of Logistics procurement for synchronized mass-driver engines, and the Terpsichore boosters from Beat 25 routed into it. It reads as an arms build-up no one can explain. What it is for is Albrecht's to say, in Beat 34 — the bible gives him one scene, and it should not be pre-empted here.
- **Fallout:** Chip leaks what they found on stream. Aysel is exposed on public channels.

### Beat 29: Aysel's Vendetta
- **Setting:** Broadcast network back-alleys.
- **Action:** Humiliated by Chip's leaks, and told by the Ministry that the team killed Ottenok (canon §8), Aysel mobilizes her private corporate strike teams to wipe out Chip's crew permanently.

### Beat 30: The Bait
- **Setting:** Abandoned broadcasting spire, Old Sector.
- **Action:** Aysel deliberately feeds false transmission coordinates regarding Langley's father to Kathay, exploiting her obsessive conspiratorial nature. Kathay sells it on as hot and rushes into the field to verify, forcing the crew to follow.

### Beat 31: The EMP Detonation — Mid-Game Climax
- **Source:** bible, Act 2 (70–75%).
- **Setting:** Industrial junction, Old Sector.
- **Action:** Aysel springs the trap. A massive wide-spectrum EMP mine knocks out all digital systems, communications, feed relays, and military AR visors across three sectors. Aysel's own squad is caught in the backfire, and so is she.

### Beat 32: The Visor Falls — The Lie Exposed
- **Setting:** The ruined industrial corridor, shrouded in emergency amber lighting.
- **Action:** The filter crashes mid-combat. Narrator's visor dies and slides off. Chip's broadcast rig flatlines.
- **The Struggle:** The narrator is in a raw physical fight with an ordinary human miner swinging industrial tools — no hitbox, no render, a man his own size (bible).
- **The Truth:** For the first time, they look at the "bugs" with bare, unassisted human eyes.
- **The Horror:** There are no aliens. There are no Nephilim. The grotesque, lumbering silhouettes shrink into ordinary human colonists and mine workers in industrial exosuits, cheap prosthetics and patched hazard gear, breathing sulfur through jury-rigged scrubbers. Nothing was ever fused or grown. The monster was the render. The roars were screams. (Canon §2.)
- **Aysel:** She sees it with them. What Ottenok was chasing was real. She will not believe it was Albrecht (canon §8).

### Beat 33: Captured
- **Source:** bible, Act 2.
- **Setting:** The same corridor, minutes later.
- **Action:** Albrecht's elite forces arrive at once to contain the leak. Everyone who saw is taken — the narrator, Chip, and Aysel. The analog footage from the blackout goes with them.

## Act 3: The interrogation and the fork

### Beat 34: The Interrogation — The Fork
- **Source:** bible, Act 3; canon §13 (the Grand Inquisitor).
- **Setting:** Albrecht's custody.
- **Action:** Albrecht confronts them face to face. The numbers; the elites' corruption; the plan to send Io into Europa and fuse the wreck into **Eden**. His one scene of self-justification. He wants agreement, not obedience.
- **Who is in the room** *(proposed)*: the narrator and Chip. Aysel is held apart, treated with respect — the hostage he respects (canon §8) — so she does not hear him, and the Route A heist stays her reveal.
- **The Fork:** permanent. Reject him (Route A) or agree (Route B). How the run feeds the fork is open: `game_design.md`, "The ending".

### Route B — Complicit, the hero (short epilogue)

### Beat B1: Epilogue — The Hero
- **Action:** A chilling time-skip. The narrator lives a lavish, decorated life as a war hero; Chip's channel is a huge success and her avatar's eyes are dead; the artificial war quietly grinds on toward Eden.
- **Aysel** *(rough, subject to change)*: she becomes the enemy. The player may kill her. If they refuse, Albrecht moves to kill her, and the player may stop him. Each outcome changes the epilogue.

### Route A — Whistleblower, the burden of truth (canon route)

### Beat 35: The Breakout
- **Action:** The narrator and Chip break out of Albrecht's custody. Aysel comes with them: she has turned, though she still blames someone around him.
- **Open:** how the separatists make contact (bible: the escape is "under separatist support").

### Beat 36: Inadmissible Truth
- **Setting:** Tank's blackout bunker.
- **Action:** Whatever footage survived the blackout and the capture is raw, but the Ministry controls the decryption keys and transmission towers. Any broadcast showing unrendered humans is instantly scrubbed as "deepfake terrorist propaganda." They need hard, knock-down evidence — proof that Albrecht knowingly ran it — and a way to put it on every screen.
- **Aysel's drive** *(proposed)*: Kathay finally cracks what Ottenok left in the locker (Beat 19). It points the way into the Ministry.

### Beat 37: The Assembly
- **Setting:** Captain Keno's back room.
- **Action:** Assembling every ally for an all-out infiltration of Ministry Headquarters:
  - **Narrator:** Field commander / Executive Producer.
  - **Chip:** The Voice / Public Catalyst.
  - **Keno:** Logistics and diversion routes.
  - **Tank:** Heavy breacher rig and EMP shields.
  - **Langley:** Point security and anti-materiel suppression.
  - **Kathay:** Master network intrusion.
  - **Aysel:** Her name, her manor's access, and her strike teams.
  - **The separatists:** The way out.

### Beat 38: The Heist — The Ministry Breach
- **Setting:** Ministry of Logistics & Broadcasting Citadel, Central Juno.
- **Action:** Infiltrating the tower during an orbital alignment celebration. Fighting through private guards and automated systems.
- **The Evidence:** The filter itself — the logistics trail from Albrecht's old desk to every visor. It proves he knowingly ran it. Aysel's reveal as much as the team's (canon §8).
- **The Encounter:** Standoff with the Enlistment Recruiter from Beat 02, now revealed as a mid-tier logistics officer who knew the truth all along. He offers the narrator a high-ranking military pardon — the counterfeit legitimate door (punchlist B5). The fork is already taken; this tests it.

### Beat 39: The Escape
- **Action:** Out of the Citadel with the evidence, under separatist support. The cost of getting out is paid here.

### Beat 40: Climax & System Broadcast
- **Setting:** Master Transmission Array, Crown of Io.
- **Action:** Final confrontation with Grand Executor Albrecht's automated security and the Ministry's forces.
- **The Feed Goes Hot:** Chip takes the stage. The unrendered footage of the colonists and the orbital collision schematics are broadcast across every screen, visor, and datapad from Io to Ganymede.
- **The Ending (bible, Route A):** Society collapses, the elites are exposed, the genocide stops, and humanity has to face its stagnation. Aysel survives as a beacon, a possible next Grand Executor.
