Cuts

Io used to be the driest object in the solar system, given it's basically one big angry volcano.
Europa has water, Io has a fire core. Every three and a half days their orbits bring them closest together.
Planetary mass railguns launch what the other needs, in "the Kiss" or... the "Hump" for the "poetic" sort.
The first poor bastards to be infected were at the old capital, Argos.


---
Infestation... Invasion... They called it many things, as if they knew what happened, but the truth is no one knows what the hell is going on.
Most called it The Revelation. The end of the world.

Whatever *it* is, it started in Argos, the colony's pride, on the fire sea of Loki Patera.

*Something* there made the poor bastards' topside suits bore into their bodies.
*Something* changed their flesh into screaming horrors of metal and muscle.
Chitin grew over their skin like armor; flesh stretched like a film over servos.
Mechanical growths burst through their skulls, fusing into their helmets.

It gives them horns.

Some called them bugs, others bots.
But I reckon what they called them told you more about the person than about the abominations.

Nobody knows what caused it, but the rumor is it's the water.

End times or not, life goes on. The fear jacked up prices and under that all-seeing eye, everyone's still gotta make a buck.
Or get drunk and punch someone.
