# Canon §8 — Cast — detail and supporting cast

Part of War Idol canon: detailed rulings a scene must obey. Index: `canon.md`.
The story bible owns the big picture and **wins any conflict**.

---

The four streamers and their arcs are in the **story bible, "Characters"**. This is the
detail and the supporting cast. The original design doc's cast table is the source both draw on.

- **The player character** is nicknamed **Meathead** or **Blockhead** and handles admin
  and security; the streamers handle media. (Default render until a name is entered.)
- **Langley** — says she's poor; **1,807 kills** on blurring fingers. Her father left
  for Io with the Pioneer Project, calls faded from daily to yearly, and she enlisted
  for revenge. E-4 pay is too low, so she mercs off-rotation. **Believes her father is
  alive.**
- **Sola O'Freyarovich** — 3rd Separate Light Assault Brigade. Luna-born, Mars-raised
  military brat. Swore with drinking buddies to avenge Io; only one who showed up. Likes
  the routine. On his first kill he felt **"the recoil."**
- **Medjed Pasha** — Free Bird Irregulars merc. Former driver with a Titan tour, four
  sons, came after bad horse-racing bets. Went merc to skip paperwork and saluting.
- **Padre Rey** — Jesuit chaplain, Mass at the front. Chip mistakes his prayers for a
  protection spell. Explains communion, and whether an alien could be baptised — which
  depends on whether it has a soul. (The moral bomb: they are people.)
- **The moon-touched soldier** — rants about the enemy's teeth: **"They're not bugs!"**
- **Aysel Terpsichore** ("Aysel of Phobos" on stream) — gyaru idol, #1 War Idol stream.
  Late 20s (up to early 30s; it doesn't show). **Not an act: this is who she is.**
  Impulsive, raucous, can't hide anything, a natural magnet for attention, good and bad.
  A natural dancer and singer. The love-to-hate "evil woman" charisma, Balalaika-type,
  but not yet that cold or that much of a killer. Chip transforms; Aysel commands.
  - **Past:** the black sheep of the Terpsichore media family. Ran away, hitchhiked to
    Phobos, drifted through music and art to spite her parents. As one of those phases she
    did a short stint as a **child soldier** — for bravery, for adventure — in a war that
    isn't this story's (Mars-side, human vs human; see open questions). She has not been a
    soldier for a long time, but **she can fight**.
  - **PTSD.** War frightens her. She acts big to blot out the fear, and has to read war
    as exciting even though it unsettles her deeply. The past is secret; the inflated
    kill counts cover a war she was scared in.
  - **Now:** Albrecht's coup more or less wiped out her family; it was blamed on the bugs.
    The survivors — support staff and minor members — called her home to the family manor
    in Juno, the new capital. **She wants revenge and blames the bugs.** The torture
    streams are fear and grief pointed at the thing the visor tells her to hate.
  - **Ottenok (outline Beat 19):**
    - She went to him to **buy the truth about her family's deaths**. He had started to
      find out it wasn't bugs. The Ministry killed him before he could sell it.
    - He shoved her into a locker as a witness, along with **a drive or code word she
      can't read**. Only Kathay can crack it later.
    - A Terpsichore pistol left at the scene **frames her**. **The team opens the locker**:
      gun up, shaking, make-up running, the first time anyone sees the fear. She bolts.
    - **Each side blames the other.** The Ministry tells her the team did it, and that
      grudge drives her hunt and her EMP trap.
  - **She doesn't know yet.** Her own EMP trap knocks out the AR (Beats 31–32) and she
    sees the truth with the team. She realises what Ottenok was chasing was real, and she
    **turns** and joins the team. The logic points at whoever gained most, but **she won't
    believe it's Albrecht**. He's soft-spoken, grounded, rational to a fault, no villain.
    She blames someone around him, or someone in the Ministry using him. **The heist
    proves he knowingly ran it.** That's her reveal as much as the team's.
  - **The irony:** she ran from her family for the same reason Albrecht destroyed them. She
    hated their corruption. She had no real love for them; she pursues revenge because
    she believes she *has* to, like her other little wars and rebellions. She feels a
    **grudging kinship with Albrecht** and understands him deep down.
  - **Albrecht and her:** he knows the family's details but never expected her to come
    home out of duty. He treats her with respect, restores the family properties and
    functions to her, and tries to bring her over to his side. He finds it awkward: Cao Cao
    with a hostage he respects. Her talent for rallying support for the war, on Io and
    off it, wins him over. They clash on procedure and principles.
  - **Her arc:** the revenge falls apart, and she has to think about who she is and what
    she wants for the first time.
  - **Route A:** she survives and becomes a beacon, a possible next Grand Executor.
  - **Route B (rough, subject to change):** she becomes the enemy. The player may kill her;
    if they refuse, Albrecht tries, and the player may stop him. Each changes the epilogue.
  - Wealthy and miserly; cute chibi avatar over the cruelty.
  - Visual design: `Characters/aysel_design.md`.
- **Kathay is a handle, not a name.** Nobody in this world goes by a real one — Chip,
  Aysel, Langley are all brands. She has an ordinary name nobody has used in years.
