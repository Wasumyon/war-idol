# War Idol — Master Story Outline & Scene Roadmap

## Structure Overview
The story follows the narrator (an amputee veteran seeking legitimacy and absolution) and Chip (a broke, neurotic VTuber seeking viral popularity as armor against abandonment) as they navigate the counterinsurgency media-industrial complex on Io.

- **Act I: The Trough (Beats 01–06)** — The Rejection, the Bar, the Brawl, the Contract.
- **Act II-A: Going Live (Beats 07–12)** — The Broadcast Van, First Stream, Rival Aysel, Tank & Langley.
- **Act II-B: The Ice & The Breach (Beats 13–22)** — Ottenok's Trap, Saving Kathay, Desert Bandits, The Father's Voice.
- **Act III: The Glitch (Beats 23–29)** — The Raid on Terpsichore, The Collision Plot, The EMP, The Visor Falls.
- **Act IV: The Grand Heist (Beats 30–32)** — Infiltrating the Ministry, The Pursuit, Standoff with the Past.

## Act I: The Trough

### Beat 01: Prologue — Battle of Chaac Patera
- **Setting:** The verdigris sulfur pits of Chaac Patera.
- **Action:** Combat drop. The APC is ripped open. The narrator is blown clear, suffering traumatic loss of his right leg. In the sensory overload and HUD glitch, he catches an unrendered glimpse of what he's firing at before blacking out.
- **Payoff:** Seeds that the war on Io is fundamentally wrong. Establishes narrator's loss, survival count (four landed, one died in rehab, three alive), and physical disability.

### Beat 02: Rejection — The Recruiter's Office
- **Setting:** Ministry Enlistment Office, High Juno.
- **Action:** Narrator attempts reenlistment to return to the field. The recruiter—a tired bureaucrat who knows the Chaac record—flatly rejects him on the grounds of his crude prosthetic leg ("with *that*?"). He is pushed toward the door and tumbled down the concrete steps into the street.
- **Mechanical Hook:** Mention of his brother; the pension payout is revealed to be a quiet settlement meant to keep him out of sight.

### Beat 03: The Foot of the Stairs — The Moon-Touched Beggar
- **Setting:** The cold curb outside the recruiting office.
- **Action:** Wounded veteran beggar sits against the wall with an open tin. Instead of internal monologue, the beggar delivers the street-level rumors and paranoia regarding the Infestation: the horns, the water scare, the Ministry's Assured Water monopoly, and frantic claims that "they aren't what the feed says."
- **Tone:** Hardboiled street reality. Narrator trades a spare voucher or cigarette for information. Establishes the public horror before showing the corporate version.

### Beat 04: The Bar — Captain Keno’s
- **Setting:** Captain Keno’s Bar, an ex-military dive catering to veterans, logistics runners, and low-tier mercs.
- **Action:** Narrator goes to drink off his refusal. Keno serves him. Chip arrives—loud, hyperactive, wearing street gear, broadcasting to a failing channel. Keno presents the riddle of the four drinks (*The Four Jewels of Io* / *The Flight*): White Cow, Peacock, Gadfly, Metamorphosis. Narrator observes Chip drinking without grasping the ceremony.

### Beat 05: The Alley — The Badge and the Blood
- **Setting:** Dark alley behind Keno’s.
- **Action:** Chip is cornered and assaulted by local goons/punks. Narrator intervenes with his cane and las-pistol; kills two attackers with brutal, practiced efficiency.
- **The Twist:** Chip wasn't screaming for rescue; she was filming. She flashes her Ministry Media Tick (Badge of Journalistic Authority). The violence sends viewer count and donations spiking through the ceiling.
- **Aftermath:** Narrator scolds her for risking her neck for numbers; she counters that being seen is the only insurance that keeps you alive.

### Beat 06: The Contract — Mutual Ruin
- **Setting:** Back room / booth at Keno’s.
- **Action:** They draft their operational pact over cheap rations.
- **The Terms:**
  1. *Elbow out the competition:* Climb the Feed rankings by any means necessary.
  2. *The Death Clause:* If either dies in the field, all gear, broadcast rights, and assets revert to the survivor.
- **Themes:** Legitimacy meets monetization. He gets his ticket back into the red zone; she gets lethal muscle that won't run.

## Act II-A: Going Live

### Beat 07: First Stream (In Media Res)
- **Setting:** Red Zone perimeter, moving at high velocity.
- **Action:** Thrown directly into the combat broadcast. Chip hypes the feed while the narrator manages defenses, dodging railgun debris and incoming missile locks while framing high-action combat shots.
- **Tutorial:** Half-tutorial. Chip and the narrator teach the controls in banter, each line fired by an event (first dodge, three dodges, first beam). They bicker over the rig's name: he calls it "Isabella", she calls it "The Big Chip".
- **Cliffhanger:** An undodgeable guided warhead locks onto the vehicle. "The Big Chip is going down!" Flashback cut.

### Beat 08: Flashback — The Loan, The Mechie & The Rig
- **Setting:** Tank’s Mechie Garage, Lower Juno.
- **Action (Earlier that Day):**
  - Meet **Tank**: stubborn, grease-stained mechanic fighting to keep an independent repair shop alive against corporate rentiers and Ministry hardware monopolies.
  - The broadcast vehicle: a battered, government-surplus APC retrofitted with heavy glass, camera gantry, and dangerous broadcast rigs.
  - The narrator secures a predatory high-interest loan to patch the cooling lines and install armor plates.
  - The rig's name: still argued, never settled.
  - Possible cameo: Langley snooping for her dad.
  - Integration: Narrator calls in an old favor to mount a salvaged APC blast engine recovered from the wreckage of Chaac Patera (boost vs super-armor trade-off).

### Beat 09: Resolution of the Strike
- **Setting:** Return to Beat 07 cliffhanger.
- **Action:** Narrator dumps power into the salvaged blast engine/super-armor. The missile detonates off the reinforced deflector; the van barrels through smoke. The stream viewers erupt; superchats flood the HUD.
- **The name:** After boosting through the final obstacle, the narrator claims naming rights: "I saved your life on the ___, and that's that!" The player fills the blank and names the rig. Chip concedes.

### Beat 10: Aftermath at Keno’s
- **Setting:** Captain Keno’s Bar.
- **Action:** Celebrating Day 1 survival and revenue split. Chip orders the flight again, demonstrating she has begun to understand Keno's drink riddle—acting less like a desperate tourist and more like an operator.

### Beat 11: Enter the Rival — Aysel Terpsichore
- **Setting:** Keno's Bar / Feed monitors.
- **Action:** The screens light up with the #1 ranked War Idol stream: **Aysel Terpsichore**. She broadcasts high-production, sadistic footage torturing captured "bugs" on camera, framing it as patriotic duty for the 8 Families.
- **Confrontation (Aysel is in the bar in person):**
  - **Kathay speaks first** — a loud stranger at the bar; the crew doesn't know her yet. She jabs at Aysel's ties to the elite and the title and pose "handed" to her by Albrecht, and may compliment up-and-coming Chip.
  - Aysel moves to strike her; Kathay flinches into a cute defensive crouch. Aysel retracts the fist and scoffs: Albrecht took charge and saved the people, there'd be no Captain Keno's without him, no shame in helping a man like that. She flaunts her wealth and calls people like Chip lookie-loos after a quick buck and cheap thrills (true of herself too; left unsaid). The pro-Albrecht men at the bar cheer and hoot.
  - **Chip**, mortified, fires back in desperation: Aysel's a sell-out, nothing without her money and connections.
  - **Kathay** drops a conspiracy that's close to the truth but a red herring: Aysel and Albrecht are in on it together. A third faction, not anti-Albrecht but sick of people throwing their weight around (Stormcloaks vs Empire), calls the cheering side bootlickers.
  - Kathay then lets slip what happened to the Terpsichore family and Aysel's sudden rise. Huge reaction from the rebellious crowd.
  - **Aysel loses it and swings.**
- **Introduction of Langley:** A tall, deadpan merc enforcer who has been watching the whole time (foreshadowed in the art as a literal shadow). She steps in, takes Aysel's punch on the cheek, and just stares at her. She watches Chip’s feed; her compliments sound like insults, but she reveals genuine tactical insight. Suffers from stage fright when facing a camera, but zero battle fright under fire. Recruitable as automated defensive gunner.
- **The brawl:** The bar erupts. Captain Keno calmly dodges, tallying who broke what and who to charge, with superhuman comic calm — another Saturday. Kathay scurries off. Aysel, shamed, declares war on Chip and her crew (just the two of them so far) and marches out as bottles and glasses fly over her as if she has a protective bubble. The MC just enjoys the catfight, to Chip's consternation.
- **Choice note (for later):** a good spot for "fake" choices that don't change the story — comic participation and effect.

### Beat 12: The Interview System & Watcher 6
- **Setting:** The Big Chip / Mobile Rig.
- **Action:** Tank installs a repurposed military ham radio / long-range intercept array.
- **The Scoop:** Chip conducts an audio interview with a ragged voice claiming to be the sole survivor of the fallen outpost *Watcher 6*. The emotional, raw broadcast breaks algorithmic records.
- **Undercurrent:** Unknown to Chip, the voice belongs to Langley's missing father.

## Act II-B: The Ice & The Breach

### Beat 13: Days 05–10 — The Broker’s Leak
- **Setting:** Transit routes through the sulfur plains.
- **Action:** Information broker **Ottenok** sells the crew an exclusive leak on an unannounced Ministry staging zone, opening lucrative new high-risk broadcast routes.

### Beat 14: Ottenok's Trap & Saving Kathay
- **Setting:** Sub-crustal mining bypass / lava tubes.
- **Action:** The tip was a death trap set to eliminate independent streamers. The crew arrives to find an ambush in progress.
- **Meeting Kathay:** They rescue **Kathay**, an obsessive, autistic hacker who also bought Ottenok's coordinates looking for suppressed Ministry data.
- **The Passage:** Under heavy fire, they stash the damaged vehicle in an abandoned deep-bore tunnel and escape on foot through caverns filled with petrified/dead "bugs."
- **Kathay's Theory:** While hiding, Kathay dumps her conspiracy lore: Albrecht's 40-year exile, the power-sharing deadlock of the 8 Founding Families, and her wild theory that the creatures are "Nephilim angels" culled from Jupiter’s Eye via ancient occult prions. (Narrator listens with quiet skepticism).
- **Stealth:** Sneaking past a dormant cluster of miners/creatures before reaching Ottenok’s bunker.

### Beat 15: Ottenok Dead
- **Setting:** Ottenok's fortified communication relay.
- **Action:** They breach the relay to find Ottenok executed at his terminal.
- **Evidence:** Left behind is a customized Terpsichore sigil pistol, planted to make it look like Aysel killed him. The Ministry did it, because he was touching the truth.
- **The locker:** the team finds Aysel hidden in a locker, a witness Ottenok put there with something only Kathay can later crack. Gun up, shaking, then she bolts. The Ministry tells her the team did it. Detail: `canon/08_cast.md`.

### Beat 16: The Ice Towers
- **Setting:** The frozen methane and sulfur ice pillars, two days out from Juno.
- **Action:** With the rig compromised and systems freezing, the crew navigates the treacherous terrain on foot and flatbed.

### Beat 17: Ambush of the Ice Raiders
- **Setting:** Narrow crevasse between the ice spires.
- **Action:** Ice Desert Raiders (desperate, unlicensed outcasts living beyond the dome networks) surround and ambush the party.

### Beat 18: Langley’s Reveal
- **Setting:** Raider kill-zone.
- **Action:** As bandits grab Chip, a warning shot cracks from the ridge. One raider steps forward, lowering a snow visor: **Langley**. She is embedded with/held by the bandits.

### Beat 19: Captives by Choice
- **Setting:** Raider dugout / cave settlement.
- **Action:** To prevent a bloodbath, Langley stages their capture, agreeing to sit in holding cells with them. She explains she tracked the bandit network following leads on her father's distress transmission. She plays the recording: her father's voice, desperate and cut short by Ministry jammers.

### Beat 20: The Geothermal Haven
- **Setting:** Deep cave volcanic spring (onsen) beneath the ice.
- **Action:** Downtime beat. The crew washes sulfur dust away, thawing out frozen prosthetics and sharing histories. Contrasts the freezing Jovian waste with human warmth.

### Beat 21: Caravan Raid & The Breakout
- **Setting:** Military supply convoy route.
- **Action:** A Terpsichore private security convoy moves through raider territory. The bandits attack; the raid turns chaotic when heavy gunships intervene.
- **The Break:** The narrator, Chip, and Langley use the firefight to hijack an armored hauler and escape back toward Juno, securing shipping manifests proving Aysel is stockpiling heavy kinetic boosters.

### Beat 22: Reorganizing the Crew
- **Setting:** Tank’s Mechie Garage.
- **Action:** The crew consolidates. Langley signs on as primary tactical enforcer. Kathay steps back from field ops to become the permanent off-site hacker and intelligence coordinator. The narrator is formally designated **Executive Producer**, controlling operations, stream routes, and asset allocation.

## Act III: The Glitch

### Beat 23: Days 15–20 — Infiltrating the Terpsichores
- **Setting:** Upper Tier Spire, Juno Grand District.
- **Action:** Disguised using counterfeit media credentials, the team infiltrates a lavish gala hosted at the Terpsichore family manor.

### Beat 24: The Sabotage & The Planetary Plot
- **Setting:** Aysel’s private server suite.
- **Action:** Kathay cracks the estate network while Chip runs social interference. The narrator infiltrates Aysel’s sanctum.
- **The Discovery:** Albrecht’s true plan is revealed. He does not care about holding Io; he plans to use synchronized mass-driver engines to desynchronize Io's orbit and collide it into Europa, combining Io's mineral core with Europa's ocean mantle to forge a single terraformed Jovian super-planet, wiping out the 8 Families in the process.

### Beat 25: Aysel's Vendetta
- **Setting:** Broadcast network back-alleys.
- **Action:** Humiliated and exposed on public channels by Chip's leaks, Aysel mobilizes her private corporate strike teams to wipe out Chip's crew permanently.

### Beat 26: The Bait
- **Setting:** Abandoned broadcasting spire, Old Sector.
- **Action:** Aysel deliberately feeds false transmission coordinates regarding Langley’s father to Kathay, exploiting her obsessive conspiratorial nature. Kathay rushes into the field to verify, forcing the crew to follow.

### Beat 27: The EMP Detonation
- **Setting:** Industrial junction, Old Sector.
- **Action:** A massive wide-spectrum EMP mine is triggered by Aysel’s ambush unit. The blast wave knocks out all digital systems, communications, feed relays, and military AR visors across three sectors. Aysel's own squad is caught in the backfire.

### Beat 28: The Visor Falls — The Lie Exposed
- **Setting:** The ruined industrial corridor, shrouded in emergency amber lighting.
- **Action:** Narrator’s visor dies and slides off. Chip’s broadcast rig flatlines.
- **The Truth:** For the first time, they look at the "bugs" with bare, unassisted human eyes.
- **The Horror:** There are no aliens. There are no Nephilim. The grotesque, lumbering silhouettes shrink into ordinary human colonists and mine workers in industrial exosuits, cheap prosthetics and patched hazard gear, breathing sulfur through jury-rigged scrubbers. Nothing was ever fused or grown. The monster was the render. The roars were screams. (Canon 2.)
- **Resolution:** The Ministry’s AR HUDs were programmed to render these desperate insurgent miners as chittering insectoid beasts to make xenocide palatable to the public feed.

### Beat 29: Inadmissible Truth
- **Setting:** Tank's blackout bunker.
- **Action:** The analog footage recorded during the blackout is raw, but the Ministry controls the decryption keys and transmission towers. Any broadcast showing unrendered humans is instantly scrubbed as "deepfake terrorist propaganda." They cannot just post it; they must seize the Master Broadcast Array at Ministry HQ to broadcast unencrypted across the entire Jovian system.

## Act IV: The Grand Heist

### Beat 30: The Assembly
- **Setting:** Captain Keno’s back room.
- **Action:** Assembling every ally for an all-out infiltration of Ministry Headquarters:
  - **Narrator:** Field commander / Executive Producer.
  - **Chip:** The Voice / Public Catalyst.
  - **Keno:** Logistics and diversion routes.
  - **Tank:** Heavy breacher rig and EMP shields.
  - **Langley:** Point security and anti-materiel suppression.
  - **Kathay:** Master network intrusion.

### Beat 31: The Ministry Breach & Standoff
- **Setting:** Ministry of Logistics & Broadcasting Citadel, Central Juno.
- **Action:** Infiltrating the tower during an orbital alignment celebration. Fighting through private guards and automated systems.
- **The Encounter:** Standoff with the Enlistment Recruiter from Beat 02, now revealed as a mid-tier logistics officer who knew the truth all along. Dialogue branch tests the narrator's loyalty: buy into a high-ranking military pardon, or burn the system to the ground.

### Beat 32: Climax & System Broadcast
- **Setting:** Master Transmission Array, Crown of Io.
- **Action:** Final confrontation with Grand Executor Albrecht's automated security and Aysel’s remnants.
- **The Feed Goes Hot:** Chip takes the stage. The unrendered footage of the colonists and the orbital collision schematics are broadcast across every screen, visor, and datapad from Io to Ganymede.
- **Ending Branches:** Determined by Player Choices throughout (Sympathy vs Drama, Hype rating, and Moral accountability).
