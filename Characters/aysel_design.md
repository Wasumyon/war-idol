# Aysel Terpsichore — design brief

Who she is lives in `canon/08_cast.md`. This file is only how she looks.

**Direction:** from field-soldier pin-up to a **gyaru idol who wears war as fashion and
pomp**. She performs propaganda; she isn't a soldier any more (she still can fight). Every
military piece is ceremonial, stolen or self-awarded. Late 20s and it doesn't show.
She is not acting: loud, impulsive, takes up the whole frame.

**Style:** the Chip sheet — thin warm-brown line, flat cel colour with one soft shadow tone,
big eyes, angular accent shapes.
**Attitude refs:** the tan gyaru (nails, rings, jacket off the shoulders, toothy sneer);
YR545 (the huge puffy jacket, lime tights).

## Head to toe
- **Hair:** honey-platinum, side ponytail to the waist, choppy face-framing strands.
- **Face:** gyaru make-up — heavy lashes, winged liner, white highlight under the eyes, red
  gloss, a sharp canine. Violet contacts. A beauty mark under one eye.
- **Piercings:** stacked ears (including a grenade-pin charm), a tongue stud (show it in the
  snarl), a navel ring, maybe an eyebrow bar.
- **Neck:** a choker built like a uniform collar tab with rank pips.
- **Top:** cream **bandeau / crop top** with tunic-style frog fastenings. Midriff bare for
  the tattoo.
- **Jacket — the centrepiece:** an oversized, floppy, **puffy cream bomber**, worn hanging off
  both shoulders and swallowing her arms (YR545 bulk). Dressed up with **parade trim** so the
  bulk reads posh, not practical:
  - gold braid frogging across the chest (the horizontal cords of a hussar jacket);
  - fringed gold epaulettes sitting on the *dropped* shoulders;
  - a crimson lining that flashes when she moves;
  - **far too many medals** pinned to the puff: hearts, stars, a cartoon skull, a
    "1,000 KILLS" badge with a little digital counter. All self-awarded.
  Historical cue: the **hussar pelisse**, a fur-trimmed jacket slung off one shoulder purely
  for show.
- **Sash:** crimson, propaganda typeface. Slogan TBD: `GIVE IT ALL` · `YOUR SACRIFICE, MY
  STAGE` · `IO LOVES YOU BACK`.
- **Hands:** long red nails with gold star decals, tally marks on one hand, rings on most
  fingers (one is the family signet), a studded cuff, one white parade glove on the mic hand.
- **Waist:** high-waisted black shorts, wide belt with the crest buckle, a hip holster that
  carries her mic.
- **Tattoo:** the Terpsichore lyre as a womb tattoo, low on the belly (see "the crest").
- **Legs:** **sulfur-lime opaque footless tights**. Io's colour, worn as a cynical nod to the audience.
- **Feet:** open-toe hard-shell sci-fi sandal-boots (see Materials). The lime tights are
  **footless**. Combat boots would say soldier; bare, pampered toes say trophy.

## Materials — sci-fi showpiece, not street clothes (settled in the ChatGPT rounds)
- Glossy, moulded surfaces: padded technical fabric on the jacket, with quilted moulded
  panels, round snap-button ports, neon lime zipper pulls, a printed serial number and
  barcode on the sleeve.
- A glossy cream sci-fi bodysuit with panel seams and a thin black harness (replaces the
  bandeau and shorts). Latex-sheen lime tights.
- **Footwear:** hard-shell sandal-boots in glossy white and black moulded plastic, with an
  ankle cuff, a number label and a gold lyre emblem. **The front is cut fully open, with bare
  toes. Not clear plastic, which reads as a retail wedge.**
- "Fashion runway, not combat gear."
- **Open:** the lower half is too bare. Either (1) a leotard covering the midriff with the
  crest printed on it, keeping the real tattoo hidden for off-camera scenes, or (2) glossy
  high-waisted shorts, with the tattoo visible above the waistband. Leaning (1).
- Latest image: `Characters/aysel_gpt_v2.png` (ChatGPT, 2nd round). The top, highlights
  and shoes are kept.

## Props
- **Mic-megaphone:** gold idol mic with a small bullhorn flare and the crest.
- **Candy cigarettes** in ration-style packaging.
- **Chibi avatar:** pastel, rounded, saluting with a wink, medals turned into stickers.

## The crest
Terpsichore is the muse of dance; her attribute is the **lyre**, with a laurel wreath.
- **Family version (formal, gold):** a lyre whose two arms curl outward at the top, a
  crossbar, strings, a **heart-shaped soundbox**, and laurel branches as wings. It goes on the
  beret badge, the belt buckle, the signet ring, the mic and the sigil pistol.
- **Her tattoo (womb tattoo):** the same lyre, inked cheaply on Phobos. The lyre's U-shape
  and outward curls already match the classic womb-tattoo silhouette. The laurel becomes
  angular shard wings, the heart is filled crimson, and there are stick-and-poke dots and a
  small star. It was an insult to her parents; since the coup it is also a memorial.

## The tell (PTSD, never on camera)
- One **real dog tag**, scuffed, tucked inside the top among the fake medals.
- She holds the mic with her **index finger laid straight along it**: trigger discipline.
- A **knuckle scar** under the rings. When she fights, she moves like she was trained.

## Sheet to draw (Chip layout: three crops, hips up)
1. **Neutral:** hand on hip, chin up, half-lidded, looking down at the viewer, jacket half off.
2. **On stream:** dancing mid-step, salute and wink, tongue out with the stud showing, mic
   held up like a torch.
3. **Snarl:** leaning in, fangs, pointing at the lens with a long nail.
4. *(optional)* **Off camera:** the same face gone blank, a thousand-yard stare, the candy
   box crushed in her fist.
