ENGINEER - design sheet, NO reference
=====================================

Same prompt, standalone. Use this when you want the design language without
uploading anything - the reference's ideas are all written into the text here.


Design sheet for a video game character: a young woman who is a back-alley
mechanic on Io, the sulfur moon of Jupiter.

Portrait format. A clean anime production design sheet: a large three-quarter
view showing the whole figure in a working stance, a full-body front view and a
back view beside it, and three or four detail callouts - head, shoulder fitting,
gauntlet, tool.

WHO SHE IS: one of the last non-corporate tradeswomen on a moon that has been
bought up by contractors. She repairs cybernetic limbs and grafted industrial
fittings. Not glamorous, not styled. Everything she wears is cheap, practical,
and has been repaired more than once.

FACE AND HEAD: short black hair, blunt and practical. Glass goggles pushed up on
her forehead - plain, dumb, scratched glass. NO AR visor, no HUD, no glowing
lenses. She is the only person on this moon who does not wear a screen over her
eyes. A grease smudge on one cheek, dirty hands. She looks like she has been
working twelve hours.

SHOULDER FITTINGS - IMPORTANT: big circular pauldrons on both shoulders, each a
sealed round port with a toothed, zipper-like rim, closed shut. These are graft
sockets - the exact hardware her customers wear - sitting on her as workwear,
closed and empty. They should read like a hardpoint on a machine, not armour.
These two discs are her silhouette.

HOSE - KEEP IT SIMPLE: one teal pneumatic hose. It runs from a small canister on
her lower back, over one shoulder, into the back of her tool. Cloth-braided,
patched once, maintained rather than purchased. One clear line, not loops.

CLOTHING: an off-white canvas work shirt worn open over a black tank. Charcoal
cargo trousers rolled at the calf. Heavy scuffed lace-up work boots. Knee
padding. A belt of mismatched pouches. Heavy work gauntlets with small round
ports on the back of the hand.

PALETTE: cold. Steel grey, off-white, charcoal, and one accent she chose
herself - teal. No orange, no pink, no yellow.

GEAR: everything she carries is unpowered, mechanical and old. No glowing
lights, no lit panels, no screens on her equipment.

TOOLS, OVERSIZED: a welding torch as her signature tool - elongated and scuffed,
the hose running into the back of it, a bright blue-white flame at the nozzle.
It should look built for someone twice her size.

HARDWARE LANGUAGE: every fitting on her - shoulder ports, gauntlet ports, tool
collars - circular, ringed, toothed, and visibly the same system. She makes this
hardware, so it appears on her too.

READABILITY: the design must still read at thumbnail size. The two shoulder
discs, the cold palette and the single hose are what identify her. Keep the
silhouette clean.

STYLE: clean anime production design art - a tidy sheet, but a weathered
subject. Flat cel shading, matte finish, no gloss or sheen. Plain light grey
background. No text, no labels, no annotation marks, no watermark.
