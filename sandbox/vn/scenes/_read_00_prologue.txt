A leg.

My leg.

It shouldn’t be there.

That’s strange… I thought losing it’d hurt more.

The eye is there. Always watching. The burning gaze of Jupiter.

Gunfire. The bunker boys are still alive. Good. That’ll give me at least a minute to fix this…
I claw at the caustic dirt and pull.

It smells like a pool.

---
My glove seal is blown. I can hear the chlorine hiss as it burns my skin, but I don’t feel it.

Bartok is screaming names on comms.
Roll call.

I know all of them.
Then mine.

But I can’t answer — can’t make a noise.
Only a little… more.

---
I was prepared to give my life up for my comrades.
But… not my leg.

I can still fight… I’m not going to let them take my home away. My dad fought for it, and his dad fought for it, and I’m sure his dad did too.

I can hear it again. The suit comms are chaos again. Screams. Shouts. Swears. Orders from hundreds of miles away.

I grab the leg.

I’ve got it now! Boys! Wait for me.
Just gotta… put it back on.

The ground buckles. Fissures rattle, spitting out realgar-red dust.

There… shouldn’t be a landtide yet?
The ground buckles again.

I hear the tortured scream of servos and look up.
A calf like a tree trunk, knee bent backwards, rears *inwards* then pins my forearm to the dirt.
Its toenails are fused at the ends into a long galv iron sickle. I dropped my leg again.

You damned bugs…

---
In a single action, drilled to automation, I unsheathe my nanoknife and jam it into the insectoid’s tarsus.
It shrieks and stumbles. Good. This one’s mine.

We laughed when we were forced to take Combatives. Who needs a knife when you got guns and drones?

But war’s not changed in a million years. Won’t change in the next million neither.

---
A blow against my helmet and all is black.

Good.

---
Visor’s down. Over comms, the chorus of screams cuts out momentarily. Mine breaks into a solo as I hack at the darkness.

The backup feed kicks in — glitched — revealing, through a storm of artifacts and blood, a knife-rent shin split open. Someone is sobbing over the static.
