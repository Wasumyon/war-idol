#
# "Good." is his tic - stoic acceptance when things go badly (Jocko). Intentional;
# do not "fix" the repetition.
#
# The stomp beat carries TWO tells about the enemy on purpose: the "tortured"
# servos and the "toenails". Each is a leak in his vocabulary that the reader cannot
# decode until the reveal, and the visor’s insectoid plates make both read as his
# style on a first pass. Do not "fix" them, and do not add a third.
#
# The monster here is the VISOR'S RENDER, not reality: the enemy are ordinary humans
# in work gear (canon 2). So the monster vocabulary - "fused", the backward knee, the
# sickle - is honest reporting of what his HUD shows him, not a leak. The leaks are
# the human words for what is underneath, and there are exactly the two above.
#
# "I dropped my leg again." is past tense in a present-tense scene ON PURPOSE. The drop
# is caused by the stomp, which has already happened by the time he looks at the foot,
# so the past marks the cause. Do not "fix" it to "I drop".
